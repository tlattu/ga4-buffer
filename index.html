Buffered GA view_promotion Demo

Modules are buffered when they are at least 80% visible in this pane. After 2 seconds of no activity (scroll/click/move/key in this pane), a view_promotion event is sent for the buffered modules. Each module is included at most once across all view events. You can only select a module after it has been viewed; on selection, the buffer is flushed first, then select_promotion is sent (once per module).
